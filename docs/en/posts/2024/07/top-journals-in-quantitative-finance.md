This article curates seven top-tier journals that quantitative finance professionals should follow, ranging from the classic Efficient Market Hypothesis to the latest research on cryptocurrencies.

---

### Journal of Finance

![L50](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/zillionare/images@main/images/2024/07/the-journal-of-finance.jpg)
Undisputedly the No. 1 journal in the field.

Launched in 1946, it is a premier academic journal published by the American Finance Association (AFA).

It attracts submissions from top scholars worldwide and serves as a critical benchmark for academic achievement and career progression.

Many early works by Nobel Laureates in Economics have been published here, including the foundations of modern finance: Modern Portfolio Theory (Markowitz), the Capital Asset Pricing Model (William Sharpe), the MM Theorem (Merton H. Miller), and the Efficient Market Hypothesis (Eugene Fama).

---

Other landmark papers include Richard Thaler’s 2017 piece, *Anomalies: The Winner’s Curse*, a pioneer in behavioral finance that demonstrates how behavioral biases of market participants affect asset prices.

In 1968, Michael Jensen’s *The Performance of Mutual Funds in the Period 1945-1964* first explicitly defined the concept of Alpha. This concept has endured to this day, acting as a beacon guiding both researchers and practitioners in quantitative finance toward a core objective.

### Journal of Financial Economics

Rolf Banz’s seminal paper, *The relationship between return and market value of common stocks* (introducing the small-cap factor), was published in this journal in 1981.

---

Eugene Fama’s five-factor model, published in 2015, also chose this journal.

### Journal of Empirical Finance

Launched in 1994.

It emphasizes empirical research, aiming to bridge the gap between financial theory and real-world data, and has become a vital platform for empirical financial studies. With a rejection rate of 85%, 40% of rejected manuscripts receive no feedback on the reasons for rejection.

### Review of Financial Studies

Published by Oxford University Press, this is a peer-reviewed academic journal.

Its impact factor was 5.814 in 2020, ranking 5th out of 110 journals in the Business and Finance categories.

---

The paper *Market Liquidity and Funding Liquidity* was first published here. On Google Scholar, this paper has been cited over 6,500 times.

### Journal of Portfolio Management

Focused on the practice and theory of portfolio management, it primarily addresses various aspects of portfolio management, including but not limited to asset allocation, risk management, quantitative strategies, market analysis, portfolio optimization, smart beta strategies, liquidity management, and global macro strategies.

Quantitative professionals in asset management should read every issue without missing a beat.

### JFQA

JFQA stands for the *Journal of Financial and Quantitative Analysis*.

---

First published in 1966, it is issued six times a year, effectively functioning as a bimonthly journal.

It covers a wide range of topics, including asset pricing, corporate finance, financial markets, market microstructure, empirical financial research, and financial econometrics.

### The Journal of Trading

A journal focusing on trading strategies, market dynamics, and trading technology, though it has since ceased publication.

A comparable publication is *The Traders' Magazine*. While not a top-tier academic journal, it stands out for its practical trading techniques, offering strong practical utility over theoretical depth, and maintains a dedicated subscriber base.
