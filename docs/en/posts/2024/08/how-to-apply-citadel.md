Citadel is a premier global hedge fund founded by Kenneth Griffin in 1990, widely considered the dream employer for many quantitative researchers.

While Citadel offers multiple roles for new graduates, an internship is often a prerequisite. Securing an internship at Citadel is highly competitive. This article outlines strategies to navigate the selection process.

We will briefly cover the three primary roles: **Investing**, **Quantitative Research**, and **Software Engineering**, with a primary focus on the Quantitative Research track.

We will share a shortcut to securing a Quant Research role and provide essential preparation materials.

## Investing Roles

Investing roles are arguably the most difficult to secure but offer exceptional compensation.

2025 undergraduate or master’s interns will receive a weekly salary of $5,300. The first week is spent staying at a Four Seasons hotel, facilitating rapid onboarding and social integration among peers.

Regarding eligibility, your major and university background are less critical; you do not need to study economics or finance, provided you have a strong interest in stock valuation.

However, the requirement is "Extraordinary"—a standard arguably higher than Harvard’s admissions criteria. Citadel claims a 1% acceptance rate compared to Harvard’s 4%. To illustrate this bar, Citadel has hired NASA astronauts.

Given this, specific advice for this role is limited. However, Citadel partners with Wonderlic for its screening process. If you are determined to apply, we recommend taking a Wonderlic prep course for approximately $50. The Wonderlic Select assesses cognitive, psychological, cultural, and logical aptitudes, helping you filter out unprepared candidates.

![](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/zillionare/images@main/images/2024/08/11-weeks-of-extraordianry-growth.jpg)
<cap>11 weeks of extraordinary growth program</cap>

Selected interns participate in an 11-week on-the-job training program designed to accelerate growth. Successful completion of this program significantly increases your chances of receiving a full-time offer.

## Software Engineering Roles

This role is relatively easier to apply for. Citadel conducts an initial screening, after which you will quickly be invited to take the HackerRank test. Since HackerRank is automated, nearly all applicants receive an invitation.

You may encounter Hard-level LeetCode problems, though some candidates report unexpectedly easy questions. Generally, consistent practice on LeetCode is beneficial. The more thoroughly you prepare, the higher your chances of success.

You can find leaked interview questions for Citadel on Glassdoor or 1point3acres, though much of this information requires payment.

## Quantitative Research and Datathon

Participating in the Datathon and achieving a high ranking is the shortcut to securing an internship in the **Quantitative Research** role. Historical data suggests low competition (based on valid submission counts), with two opportunities per year.

The competition is organized by Correlation One (C1). C1 was founded by former hedge fund manager Rasheed Sabar and specializes in corporate training and talent development.

Their clients include the DoD, Amazon, Citadel, and Point72. Leveraging the founder’s professional network, C1 secured the opportunity to host competitions and recruit for Citadel and Point72. You may also explore their training and recruitment programs on their website.

![](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/zillionare/images@main/images/2024/08/correlation-one.jpg)
<cap>Correlation One</cap>

The Datathon is exclusive to current students, requiring a school email for registration. After online registration, you must complete a 90-minute online assessment, which includes psychological, value-based, and technical components.

Results are announced three days before the competition begins. This is followed by a networking session where you must form a team or join an existing one. The Datathon is a collaborative project, typically requiring teams of four.

Upon commencement, you will receive the problem statement and dataset from the organizers (**we have collected historical questions, datasets, and team submissions; see the link at the end of this article**). You must select a problem to research and submit a report within seven days detailing your findings.

This process may feel unfamiliar to students in mainland China, but for overseas students, such collaboration and team presentations are routine. Mainland students should practice these skills, as seven days can feel insufficient without prior experience.

## Women’s Benefits

In addition to the standard Datathon, there is a dedicated Women’s Datathon. The next edition is scheduled for January 10 next year, making now an ideal time to prepare.

Note that this year’s Women’s Datathon will be held in-person and is restricted to students currently enrolled in the US and Canada.

## Datathon Strategy

While the Datathon appears to test hard data analysis skills, understanding the rules and mastering team collaboration are equally critical. Culturally, Citadel places a high value on collaboration.

1.  **Team Composition:** Ensure all team members use the same programming language. Incompatible codebases cannot be aggregated.
2.  **Deliverables:** Although Citadel does not restrict programming languages or tools, the final report must be in PDF, PPT, or HTML format. If your PDF contains formulas, you must also provide the LaTeX source code. Given the seven-day deadline, familiarity with these tools is essential. Alternatively, ensure your team includes a member with these skills.
3.  **Submission Standards:** The Datathon is a virtual competition, so there is no live presentation environment. Strictly adhere to all submission guidelines.
4.  **Report Clarity:** Due to the lack of live presentation, your report must be exceptionally clear. Review it from an outsider’s perspective to ensure it conveys a coherent impression.
5.  **Tools:** Familiarize yourself with Jupyter Notebooks and pandas (if using Python). These are officially recommended for quickly exploring the provided datasets.
6.  **Data Augmentation:** Incorporating additional data demonstrates your ability to solve problems beyond the provided scope. Familiarize yourself with various datasets beforehand. If you need to scrape data, ensure you are proficient in web scraping, as this is a serial process; other work cannot proceed until data is acquired.
7.  **Visualization:** Visualization is crucial. If you use Python, practice `matplotlib` and `seaborn` regularly.

1.  Can restaurant stock prices be predicted based on meat production?
2.  Does sugar pricing affect young people’s consumption of sugary drinks? If so, are there regional variations?
3.  Is there a correlation between dips in meat production and unemployment rates?
