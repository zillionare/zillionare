### Three Departments Implement First-Home Mortgage Interest Subsidy: 1% Annual Subsidy, Capped at 1 Million RMB per Household

The Ministry of Finance, the People's Bank of China, and the National Financial Regulatory Administration jointly issued a notice providing fiscal subsidies for eligible first-home mortgage borrowers. The policy applies to homes with a floor area under 120 square meters and a price below 1.5 million RMB. It takes effect from October 1, 2026, for an initial term of one year. Subsidy funds are shared 90% by the central government and 10% by local governments. ### A 1 Percentage Point Rise in 10-Year Treasury Yields Over 12 Months Correlates with S&P 500 Declines Only When Inflation Exceeds 3%

Does a rapid rise in long-term rates inevitably pressure stocks, or is inflation the true driver? This study analyzes data from January 1963 to August 2026, identifying 20 periods where the 10-year US Treasury yield rose by at least 1 percentage point over 12 months. It then groups these periods based on whether US CPI remained below 3% and tracks S&P 500 performance. Results show all declines occurred in high-inflation groups. In the six periods with CPI below 3%—including 1994, 2013, and 2021—the S&P 500 rose every time. This indicates that yield-driven market impacts are not universal but tightly bound to inflation levels. <sup>[15](https://quantocracy.com/recent-quant-links-from-quantocracy-as-of-09282026/)</sup>

### Follow the Cash: Microstructure Analysis of the US Repo Market

This paper provides an in-depth analysis of the US repurchase agreement (Repo) market’s microstructure, comparing it with short-term money markets like the federal funds market, and highlighting the repo market’s significantly larger scale. By tracing cash flows, it explains the trading structure choices of different participants: large cash lenders typically use third-party agent settlement, inter-dealer markets rely on central counterparty clearing, and leveraged investors prefer bilateral trading. The study aims to reveal the cost-benefit mechanisms shaping current market structures, helping quant practitioners understand how market infrastructure impacts trade execution and liquidity management. <sup>[16](https://libertystreeteconomics.newyorkfed.org/2026/09/follow-the-cash-microstructure-of-repo-markets/)</sup>

### Investment Banker Warned for Remote Work Interview: Balancing Safety and Pace in Job Hopping

The candidate’s job-hopping channel began with a headhunter’s outreach. After an initial phone discussion with a major bank, he received an invitation for the first round of in-person interviews for an ECM (Equity Capital Markets) role. The bottleneck was not capability but scheduling—he had used his remote work days twice this month for interviews, prompting his supervisor to issue a formal warning, equating remote work directly with "preparing to leave the team."

What should one do? Here are community suggestions:

First, when work status is tied to visas or residency qualifications, job-hopping pace should prioritize maintaining current employment status as a hard constraint. It is better to slow down rounds than to use easily detectable leave requests to secure a meeting. Interview invitations can be postponed, but breaking employment status is hard to repair quickly.

Second, if the target role is unclear, do not bear the risk of unemployment for a single first-round interview. First, assess whether ECM aligns with your long-term path before deciding if the risk is worth taking.

Third, front-load coordination costs: Before agreeing to an in-person interview, confirm with the headhunter if it can be changed to a phone or video call, scheduled during non-working hours, or if multiple rounds can be compressed to reduce exposure. Also, clarify the process’s confidentiality expectations, letting the intermediary negotiate timing and format with the candidate. <sup>[12](https://www.reddit.com/r/FinancialCareers/comments/1wskmrg/need_help_deciding_to_attend_in_person_interview/) <sup>[1](https://www.reddit.com/r/FinancialCareers/comments/1wskmrg/need_help_deciding_to_attend_in_person_interview/)</sup>
