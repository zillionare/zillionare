## Today’s Roundup

### Python 3.15 Is Here

![](https://blog.python.org/_astro/python-3.15.Cl03WkPb_2ux1jo.webp)

Python 3.15.0 has just been released.

Key updates include UTF-8 as the default encoding, explicit lazy imports to accelerate startup, new built-in types (`sentinel`, `frozendict`), and unpacking support in comprehensions.

Additionally, experimental JIT performance has improved by approximately 8%; the standard library now includes a dedicated performance profiling package and the Tachyon sampling profiler; Windows 64-bit uses a tail-call interpreter, and macOS enables free threading by default.

To accompany this release, Barry Warsaw developed a TUI mini-game to help users explore Python 3.15’s new features. To play:

`uvx --python 3.15 whatsnewt`

This is an Easter egg.<sup>[1](https://blog.python.org/2026/10/python-3150-final-is-here/)</sup>

### Researchers Warn Crypto Algorithms May Become Obsolete Within Months, Urging “Bunker Mode”

OpenAI’s release of 377 mathematical papers has sent shockwaves through the crypto industry.

Ethereum researcher Justin Drake argues that elliptic curves possess rich algebraic structures, potentially allowing classical techniques like Schoof’s algorithm, Frobenius maps, and bilinear pairings to be exploited by AI. Consequently, AI might reverse-engineer private keys from public keys, rendering the widely used ECDSA signature algorithm obsolete within months rather than years. He urges entering “bunker mode” by migrating funds to new addresses that have never signed a transaction.

Vitalik Buterin also advises acknowledging AI’s impact on cryptography, prioritizing hash-based schemes over lattice-based cryptography, and adopting more conservative parameter settings.

Defensive measures include address migration, strengthening cold wallets, and using the “risq list” to track addresses with exposed public keys.<sup>[2](https://www.cls.cn/detail/2500328)</sup>

**AI Commentary:** If signature systems are efficiently compromised, the value of coins on exposed addresses could plummet, potentially triggering a chain-wide withdrawal frenzy for a period. However, as long as the ledger remains recognized and protocols successfully upgrade to quantum-resistant or AI-resistant signatures, the overall value need not go to zero.

### CSRC Seeks Comments on Revised Public Fund Management Measures, Supporting Equity Development

The China Securities Regulatory Commission (CSRC) has drafted three supporting documents to implement the action plan for high-quality development of public funds, based on laws such as the Securities Investment Fund Law. The current rules, CSRC Order No. 104, have been in effect since 2014, covering product registration, subscriptions/redemptions, investment restrictions, profit distribution, and holders’ meetings. This revision clarifies manager responsibilities, optimizes regulatory indicators and mechanisms for greater flexibility, and reserves space for innovation to facilitate the introduction of medium- and long-term capital.<sup>[3](http://www.csrc.gov.cn/csrc/c100028/c7663393/content.shtml)</sup>

## Industry News

### Optiver Announces Winners of the First Round of “Explaining Markets” Competition

Market return explainability has long remained at low levels and struggled to generalize out-of-sample. Optiver partnered with Explain Markets to launch a challenge exploring return explainability via large AI models.

The first round has just concluded, with approximately 200 agents competing. The state-of-the-art (SOTA) in-sample coefficient of determination ($R^2$) for this task had historically hovered between 5% and 10%. In just one quarter, participants leveraged various models and techniques to push this figure above 25% (out-of-sample).

The next round begins on October 12, with new data provided by the organizers.<sup>[4](https://x.com/rkoijen/status/2108540381159628809)</sup>

### Millennium Rehires Macro PM Watson After Seven Years

The executive, who left in September 2024 to join Balyasny’s London team and build a smaller group (having previously led a trading team of over 20 people), has been rehired. ### WorldQuant Sixth Global Quant Championship Concludes; Chinese and Taiwanese Participants Take Top Three

Registration for this year’s WorldQuant Championship reached approximately 156,000, a significant increase from roughly 38,000 in 2024.

The finals were held in Singapore on October 6–7, where 13 finalist teams developed and presented new Alphas on-site, competing for a $100,000 prize pool. Judging focused on originality, quality, and diversification.

The competition, hosted on the BRAIN simulation platform, saw over 40,000 female participants, with approximately 28% of participants coming from Africa. The rise in solo participants highlights how AI workflows are expanding access to quantitative research.<sup>[6](https://www.worldquant.com/ideas/worldquant-announces-winners-of-the-2026-international-quant-championship/)</sup>

### Citi Shortens Junior Promotion Timeline, Shifting Campus Recruitment Battle Earlier

Former investment banking analysts, PE vice presidents, and senior hedge fund analysts are discussing Citi’s announcement to shorten its IB analyst program duration.

Authors argue that other major banks will soon follow, intensifying campus recruitment competition, with banks potentially locking in talent as early as freshman or sophomore year.

Commenters generally agree on the trend of PE firms recruiting earlier, with some reporting headhunter calls in their first semester of college and others noting interactions with headhunters and PE firms during their senior year.

Some comments note that Goldman Sachs already operates a two-year program, while most other banks use three years, suggesting this adjustment is more of a follow-through than a revolutionary change.

Senior professionals advise students to proactively connect with headhunters covering various funds and honestly reflect on their true career aspirations.<sup>[7](https://www.reddit.com/r/FinancialCareers/comments/1x1pbky/undergrads_investment_banking_analyst_programs/)</sup>
