### Python 3.15 Just Released!

![](https://blog.python.org/_astro/python-3.15.Cl03WkPb_2ux1jo.webp)

Python 3.15.0 has just been released.

Key updates include UTF-8 as the default encoding, explicit lazy imports to accelerate startup, and new built-in types `sentinel` and `frozendict`. List comprehensions now support unpacking.

### Researcher Warns Encryption Algorithms May Fail in Months, Urges "Bunker Mode"

OpenAI’s simultaneous release of 377 mathematical papers has sent shockwaves through the crypto industry.

Ethereum researcher Justin Drake argues that elliptic curves possess rich algebraic structures, potentially allowing classic techniques like Schoof, Frobenius, or bilinear pairings to be leveraged by AI. AI might reverse-engineer private keys from public keys, causing the widely used ECDSA signature algorithm to fail in months rather than years. He urges entering "bunker mode" and moving funds to new addresses that have never signed a transaction.

Vitalik Buterin also advises acknowledging AI’s impact on cryptography, prioritizing hash-based schemes over lattice cryptography with more conservative parameters.

---

Defensive measures include address migration, strengthening cold wallets, and using the "risq list" to track addresses with exposed public keys.<sup>[2](https://www.cls.cn/detail/2500328)</sup>

**AI Commentary:** If the signature system is efficiently compromised, the value of coins on exposed addresses would plummet, potentially triggering a wave of on-chain withdrawals for a period. However, as long as the ledger remains recognized and the protocol upgrades to quantum-resistant or AI-resistant signatures, the overall value need not go to zero.

### CSRC Seeks Comments on Revised Public Fund Management Measures, Supporting Equity Development

The China Securities Regulatory Commission (CSRC) has drafted three supporting documents to implement the action plan for high-quality development of public funds, in accordance with the Securities Investment Fund Law and other regulations.

---

The current rules, CSRC Order No. 104, have been in effect since 2014, covering product registration, subscriptions/redemptions, investment restrictions, profit distribution, and holders’ meetings. This revision clarifies manager responsibilities, optimizes regulatory indicators and mechanisms to enhance flexibility, and reserves space for innovation to facilitate the introduction of medium- and long-term capital.<sup>[3](http://www.csrc.gov.cn/csrc/c100028/c7663393/content.shtml)</sup>

### Optiver Announces Winners of First "Explaining Markets" Competition

The explainability of asset returns has long remained at a low level and difficult to generalize out-of-sample. Optiver partnered with Explain Markets to launch a challenge: using AI to explore the explainability of returns.

---

The first round has just concluded, with approximately 200 agents participating. For years, the state-of-the-art (SOTA) in-sample R² for this task was only 5% to 10%. In just one quarter, participants leveraged various models and techniques to push this above 25% (out-of-sample results).

The next round begins on October 12, with new data provided by the organizers.<sup>[4](https://x.com/rkoijen/status/2108540381159628809)</sup>

### WorldQuant 6th Global Quant Championship Concludes; Chinese and Taiwanese Players Take Top Three

The registration for this WorldQuant event reached approximately 156,000, a significant increase from the ~38,000 in 2024.

The finals were held in Singapore on October 6–7, where 13 finalist teams developed and presented new Alpha strategies on-site, competing for a $100,000 prize pool. Judging focused on originality, quality, and diversification.

---

The competition was conducted on the BRAIN simulation platform. Over 40,000 women participated, with approximately 28% of participants from Africa. The rise in solo participants highlights how AI workflows are expanding the reach of quantitative research.<sup>[6](https://www.worldquant.com/ideas/worldquant-announces-winners-of-the-2026-international-quant-championship/)</sup>

### Citi Shortens Junior Promotion Timeline; Campus Recruitment War Shifts Earlier

Former investment banking analysts, PE vice presidents, and senior hedge fund analysts are discussing Citi’s announcement to shorten the IB analyst program duration.

The author believes other major banks will soon follow, making campus recruitment more competitive, with banks potentially locking in talent as early as their freshman or sophomore years.

---

Commenters generally agree on the trend of PE headhunting moving earlier. Some report being contacted by headhunters in their first semester of freshman year, while others note that some students engage with headhunters and PE firms in their senior year.

One comment points out that Goldman Sachs already operates a two-year program, while most other banks run three-year programs, suggesting this adjustment is more of a follow-through than a revolutionary change.

A senior professional advises students to proactively connect with headhunters covering various funds and to honestly reflect on their true career aspirations.<sup>[7](https://www.reddit.com/r/FinancialCareers/comments/1x1pbky/undergrads_investment_banking_analyst_programs/)</sup>
