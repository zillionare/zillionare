### Python 3.15 Has Just Released!

![](https://blog.python.org/_astro/python-3.15.Cl03WkPb_2ux1jo.webp)

---

Python 3.15.0 has just been released.

**Key updates:** UTF-8 is now the default encoding; explicit lazy imports have been introduced to accelerate startup; new built-in types include `sentinel` and `frozendict`; and comprehensions now support unpacking.

### Researcher Warns Crypto Algorithms May Become Obsolete Within Months, Urges "Bunker Mode"

OpenAI's release of 377 mathematical papers has sent shockwaves through the crypto industry.

Ethereum researcher Justin Drake argues that elliptic curves possess rich algebraic structures, potentially allowing classic techniques like Schoof's algorithm, Frobenius endomorphism, and bilinear pairings to be exploited by AI. AI might reverse-engineer private keys from public keys, rendering the widely used ECDSA signature algorithm obsolete within months rather than years. He urges entering "bunker mode" and moving funds to new addresses that have never signed a transaction.

Vitalik Buterin also advises acknowledging AI's impact on cryptography, prioritizing hash-based schemes over lattice-based cryptography with more conservative parameters.

---

Defensive measures include address migration, strengthening cold wallets, and using the "risq list" to track addresses with exposed public keys.<sup>[2](https://www.cls.cn/detail/2500328)</sup>

**AI Commentary:** If the signature system is efficiently compromised, the value of coins on exposed addresses could plummet, potentially triggering a chain-wide withdrawal frenzy. However, as long as the ledger remains valid and protocols can upgrade to quantum-resistant or AI-resistant signatures, the overall value need not go to zero.

### CSRC Seeks Comments on Revised Public Fund Management Measures to Support Equity Development

The China Securities Regulatory Commission (CSRC) has drafted three supporting documents to implement the action plan for high-quality development of public funds, based on laws such as the Securities Investment Fund Law.

---

The current regulations, CSRC Order No. 104, have been in effect since 2014, covering product registration, subscriptions/redemptions, investment restrictions, profit distribution, and holder meetings. This revision clarifies manager responsibilities, optimizes regulatory indicators and mechanisms to enhance flexibility, and reserves space for innovation to facilitate the introduction of medium- and long-term capital.<sup>[3](http://www.csrc.gov.cn/csrc/c100028/c7663393/content.shtml)</sup>

## Big Tech & Industry Moves

### Optiver Announces Winners of the First Round of "Explaining Markets" Competition

The explainability of asset returns has long remained at a low level and difficult to generalize out-of-sample. Optiver partnered with Explain Markets to launch a challenge: using AI to explore return explainability.

---

The first round of the competition has just concluded, with approximately 200 agents participating. For years, the state-of-the-art (SOTA) in-sample R² for this task was only 5% to 10%. In just one quarter, participants leveraged various models and techniques to push this figure above 25% (including out-of-sample results).

The next round will begin on October 12, with new data provided by the organizers.<sup>[4](https://x.com/rkoijen/status/2108540381159628809)</sup>

### Millennium Re-hires Macro PM Watson, Who Spent Seven Years at the Firm

The executive, who joined Balyasny's London team in September 2024 to lead a smaller squad after previously managing a team of over 20 traders, has been re-hired by Millennium.

---

### WorldQuant's 6th Global Quant Championship Concludes; Chinese and Taiwanese Players Take Top Three

Registration for this year's WorldQuant event reached approximately 156,000, a significant increase from the ~38,000 in 2024.

The finals were held in Singapore on October 6–7, where 13 finalist teams developed and presented new Alpha models on-site, competing for a $100,000 prize pool. Judging focused on originality, quality, and diversification.

---

The competition was conducted on the BRAIN simulation platform. Over 40,000 women participated, with approximately 28% of participants coming from Africa. The rise in individual participants highlights how AI workflows are broadening participation in quantitative research.<sup>[6](https://www.worldquant.com/ideas/worldquant-announces-winners-of-the-2026-international-quant-championship/)</sup>

### Citi Shortens Junior Promotion Timeline, Campus Recruitment War Moves Earlier

Former investment banking analysts, PE vice presidents, and senior hedge fund analysts are discussing Citi's announcement to shorten the Investment Banking (IB) analyst program timeline.

The author argues that other major banks will likely follow suit, intensifying campus recruitment competition. Banks may even start locking in talent during their freshman and sophomore years.

---

Commenters generally agree on the trend of earlier PE poaching. Some report being contacted by headhunters in their sophomore year, while others note that some students engage with headhunters and PEs as early as their senior year.

One comment points out that Goldman Sachs already operates a two-year program, with most other banks using three years; this adjustment is more of a follow-through than a revolutionary change.

A senior professional advises students to get to know headhunters covering various funds early on and to honestly reflect on their true career aspirations.<sup>[7](https://www.reddit.com/r/FinancialCareers/comments/1x1pbky/undergrads_investment_banking_analyst_programs/)</sup>
