### Fed Rate Hike Prices in Further Tightening, Accelerating Yen Carry Unwind

On September 16, the Federal Reserve announced a 25-basis-point rate hike, a move already fully anticipated by the market. Bond markets have priced in expectations for continued tightening. Post-hike, developed-market bond curves show short-end yields rising while long-end yields remain stable. ### 14 Intraday Momentum Signals for MNQ Futures Fail Execution Cost Tests

This study investigates a core question: whether common intraday momentum signals constructed from OHLCV data can generate tradable advantages in Micro E-mini Nasdaq 100 (MNQ) futures after deducting real execution costs. Methodologically, the authors used five-minute data from 947 trading days between 2021 and 2025, applying an extended window forward-validation (walk-forward) approach to 14 signals. Each signal was required to meet five criteria, including an out-of-sample net return t-statistic of at least 2.0 and no fewer than 30 out-of-sample trades. Key results are not disclosed in the abstract; the original text truncates at the standard-setting phase, providing no data on which signals passed, the number of passers, or specific values for net returns and Sharpe ratios. The significance lies in the study’s systematic falsification approach, offering a rigorous post-cost evaluation framework for intraday momentum strategies in MNQ futures. However, the applicability of these conclusions is limited to this specific instrument, sample period, and execution cost assumptions, and should not be extrapolated to other assets or longer holding periods. <sup>[5](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.04004)</sup>

### Optimal Entry and Exit Thresholds for Variance Swaps: A Closed-Form Solution for Perpetual Contracts

Variance swaps are convenient instruments for trading vega and convexity, and Cboe has listed related contracts. However, clear rules for when to establish or close positions remain lacking. This paper addresses perpetual, continuously settled variance swap contracts, solving for optimal entry and exit timing outside the pricing measure. The two thresholds are characterized as the unique roots of a smooth-pasting equation, with closed-form expressions provided using confluent hypergeometric functions. The abstract does not disclose results, nor does it provide quantitative metrics such as sample periods, returns, Sharpe ratios, or t-values; the authors note only that the problem lacks substantive content under the pricing measure. This rule applies to continuously settled perpetual variance swaps, providing a theoretical basis for traders to set entry and exit boundaries. However, its applicability to discretely settled or finite-term contracts remains to be tested. <sup>[6](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19102)</sup>

### 16 Tips from a Senior MD to Young Bankers: Learning Real Trading

A managing director who started in large bank mergers and now leads an industry coverage team summarized 16 tips for young bankers. The core message: participate in real transactions, as learning primarily happens within projects. Promotion does not reduce competition; there is always someone above you. Consistent output proves oneself better than overnight overtime. Understand the team’s true objectives, not just immediate tasks. Deepen expertise in one industry, product, or transaction type early on, rather than being mediocre in everything. Viewing peers as rivals is short-sighted; the financial circle is small, and colleagues, clients, mentors, and friends are worth long-term cultivation. Opportunities to work abroad or in other markets will change how you view business. Read books outside finance; do not let trading and market data consume your entire life. Do not let work define your entire personality; in cities like New York, London, and Hong Kong, you must truly live. The original text does not elaborate on entry paths, interview preparation, or compensation details. <sup>[8](https://www.reddit.com/r/FinancialCareers/comments/1wjb4m6/16_things_an_md_thinks_younger_bankers_should_know/)</sup>
