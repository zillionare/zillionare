## Enrollment and Learning Environment

!!! abstract "How do I take the course?"
    The course combines videos, Notebook-based courseware, and Q&A support. All video lessons are already recorded and uploaded to Lizhi Weike (荔枝微课).

    The course has 21 lessons, totaling 22 hours of video.

    Course materials are delivered as Jupyter Notebook documents hosted on servers we provide. Once you purchase the course, we assign you an account and spin up a dedicated computing environment just for you. There you can read and run our notebooks, and create your own as well.

    Data and dependencies are already configured in that environment. Code runs out of the box, and you are free to modify and debug it.

!!! abstract "How long does it take to finish the course?"
    The course has 21 chapters and 22 hours of video. How long it takes depends mainly on your own schedule. At one lesson per day, you should finish within three weeks.<br><br>
    Course videos are free to watch permanently. The dedicated courseware server is available until June 30, 2027.<br><br>The courseware server is for learning purposes only and may not be used as a cloud server.


!!! abstract "Who is this course for, and what are the prerequisites?"
    See the prerequisites listed in the [course introduction](/articles/course/factor-ml/intro/).


!!! abstract "Do you provide a learning environment?"
    We run a server cluster with 192 CPU cores and 256GB of RAM for students. Log in through your browser and you can study and run our example code online. The environment ships with daily bar market data for factor analysis. For other data, you can pull it through the high-privilege Tushare account we purchased.


!!! abstract "What are the advantages of your learning environment? Can I set up my own?"
    Our course examples rely on dedicated data interfaces. To build the environment locally, you would need to modify those interfaces and supply your own data. The interfaces are simple, and detailed tutorials are provided.


!!! abstract "Can I download your data and use it locally?"
    All data used in the course environment is **purchased** from third parties, and our agreements prohibit redistribution or resale. So we cannot promise to provide data downloads.

    We offer a course preview environment containing some of the lessons (videos, notebooks, and exercises). Add the teaching assistant on WeChat at quantfans_99 to get the link.
